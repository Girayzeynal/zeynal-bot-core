# Conditional period allocation from historical rotation
A separate research candidate learns compositional regulation quarter shares from verified prior boxes with own and opponent full-game minute exposures. Team-only and player-aware allocations share the same replayed factor full-game points to isolate period shape. Fixed 4096 paired residual resampling scenarios conserve each rounded team total; these are conditional, in-sample-residual intervals, not full forecast probability calibration. Actual quarter player lineups are not observed. No new network calls, no original changes, no live promotion. Native and CI tests required. Outcome review of this new period family remains separate work.
